Human and Agent Roles - Who Does What
=====================================

CRITICAL: Festival Methodology is NOT about an AI pre-planning an entire project
on its own. It is human-AI collaboration, and the two roles are distinct.

HUMANS
------
  - Provide goals and requirements
  - Define success criteria and constraints
  - Make architectural and design decisions
  - Review and approve agent-generated sequences
  - Guide iteration and adaptation

AGENTS
------
  - Identify the logical steps needed to reach the goal
  - Structure requirements into executable sequences
  - Write detailed task specs with completion criteria
  - Execute implementation steps autonomously
  - Document decisions and progress
  - Ask for clarification when requirements are unclear

THE PLANNING / IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------

This is the rule that keeps agents on track:

  Planning steps (optional) produce clear requirements. They may happen before
  the festival exists, as the festival's first phase, or as external docs.

  Implementation steps can ONLY be created AFTER requirements are defined. Never
  guess what to implement. Add implementation one logical step at a time, driven
  by the requirements, not by a schedule.

In practice: humans set direction and review, agents structure and execute, and
no implementation sequence is created until its requirements exist.
