Festival Chains - Dependencies Between Festivals
================================================

A chain links festivals that depend on each other, so a downstream festival does
not start until its upstream festivals are complete. Chains live under
festivals/chains/.

Use a chain when one body of work must finish before another can begin, and each
is large enough to be its own festival.

COMMANDS
--------

  fest chain create <name>       Create a new chain
  fest chain add <festival>      Add a festival to a chain
  fest chain list                List all chains
  fest chain status              Show chain status and progress
  fest chain graph               Visualize the dependency graph
  fest chain check               Check whether a festival is unblocked
  fest chain validate            Validate the chain
  fest chain complete            Complete and archive the chain

`fest chain check` is the gate: it tells an agent whether the current festival's
upstream dependencies are satisfied yet.
